# 0008 — Fly the X-wing in its own strip under the top bar

Status: accepted (round 5)

In the context of R21, which asks that the X-wing silhouette (R13) fly only through an empty strip of the screen and never pass over text, after rounds 2–4 let it cross the whole page 16 % from the top, over headings, cards and facts,
facing the options of a dedicated sky strip fixed under the top bar, with the page and sidebar moved down by its height; making the droids' ground strip at the bottom (U2) taller, about 80 px, and flying the X-wing above the droids in it; flying it through the empty space inside the top bar, between the brand and the search box; and working out at run time, in JavaScript, which band of the screen has no text,
we decided for a dedicated strip: `.sky` is an opaque black band right under the sticky top bar, 40 px tall (32 px below 768 px), full width, with a grey line at the bottom; `.page` and `#sidebar` start below it (`--sky`); the X-wing is drawn 112 × 32 px (84 × 24 px on phones) inside it with its laser bolts, on the unchanged R17 cycle; with reduced motion the strip is hidden and `--sky` is 0,
and neglected the taller ground strip (it costs less height in all, but an X-wing flying along the ground beside walking droids looks wrong, and a strip of 80 px at the bottom of a 812 px phone is a lot to give up while reading), the top bar's empty space (there is none at 375 px, where the search box fills the bar, and on wide screens its width changes with the window, so the X-wing would still cross the brand or the search box), and a computed empty band (it needs JavaScript measuring the layout on every page and resize, which goes against the CSS-only animations of decisions 0003, 0005 and 0007, and a band that is empty on one page is not on the next),
to achieve an X-wing that, on every page and every screen width, crosses only a band that never holds text: text that scrolls up passes behind the opaque strip and the top bar, never under the X-wing, and R21 can be checked by tests that read `styles.css` and `index.html`, like the R8 tests (decision 0006),
accepting that the strip takes 40 px (32 px on a phone) of height on every view, and is empty for about 5 minutes of every 5.5-minute cycle (R17) while the X-wing rests; together with the ground strip, a phone gives 80 px of its height to the silhouettes.
